About

Bridging simulation fidelity and materials physics.

CFD engineer turned materials scientist. Currently at TUM, open to full-time and Hiwi/working-student roles in Munich.

Simulation engineer with four years of industrial R&D experience in rotating turbomachinery, internal cooling flows, and reacting-flow systems. At Honeywell I learned to identify the governing physics and balance performance, manufacturability, and cost — but also hit a limit: fluid simulations tell only half the story. A sound design needs equal understanding of how the material responds.

I'm completing an M.Sc. in Materials Science & Engineering at TUM to close that gap and build coupled models whose predictions hold up against measured data. Thesis work on hydrofoil-based tidal energy systems combines CFD, cable-dynamics coupling, and prototype validation — the same discipline of model-against-measurement that defines my industrial work. Coursework in nonlinear continuum mechanics, multiscale modelling, uncertainty quantification, and experimental fluid mechanics rounds out the theory.
